Event Branding, Large Format Printing
& Digital Signage Solutions

Every event tells a story, the moment guests walk in, the atmosphere, the visuals, the branded details that make them feel part of something bigger. At Visibo, we design and execute that story to be bold, seamless, and unforgettable.

From corporate summits that demand authority to product launches that spark excitement, we create immersive brand worlds where every print element and digital screen work together.

What We Brand

  • Venue & Stage: main backdrops, step-and-repeat walls, stage skirting, lectern skins, side banners
  • Wayfinding & Spaces: directional signage, zone IDs, floor decals, registration desks, help desks
  • Attendee Items: badges, lanyards, wristbands, tote bags, programs, menu boards
  • Digital & Screens: LED walls, kiosks, touch screens, digital agendas, countdowns, lower-thirds, sponsor loops
  • Photo & Social: media walls, photo booths, branded frames, AR filters, QR landing pages
  • Experiential Touchpoints: demo pods, product corners, interactive zones, expo booths
