Clean and Effortless Content
Management System

Unlike many displays, our monitors don’t require connecting a
bulky laptop to play your content. Instead, we discretely attach a
mini PC (the size of an iPhone) to the back of the display.
You control your content using a wireless keyboard and trackpad.
And with our remote cloud-based content update system, we can pre-schedule
your content so that your display updates with different
messages at ideal times during your event.
